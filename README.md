# LLM Quality Survey

Working repository for:

**Towards End-to-End Quality Assessment of LLM-Based Systems: A Survey of Quality Dimensions, Evaluation Methods, and Integration**

## Structure

- main.tex: ACM journal entry point
- sections/: article sections
- references.bib: verified bibliography only
- figures/: final taxonomy, review flow, and quantitative figures

## Working page budget

The manuscript is being developed around a target of approximately **30 ACM-formatted pages including references**, leaving some margin below the journal limit.

- Abstract + Introduction: ~2 pages
- Problem formulation + Research Questions: ~2 pages
- Review Methodology: ~3 pages
- Taxonomy: ~2.5 pages
- Evidence synthesis by quality dimension: ~8 pages
- Evaluation methods: ~4 pages
- Integration/end-to-end analysis: ~3 pages
- Research gaps and directions: ~2 pages
- Conclusion: ~0.5 page
- References: ~3 pages

These are working targets, not hard per-section limits. Space should move toward evidence synthesis and away from background if the final bibliography grows.

## Current status

A first substantive draft now exists for the Introduction, problem formulation and scope, Research Questions, Review Methodology, and the conceptual taxonomy. Skeleton sections are also in place for the six quality dimensions, evaluation methods, integration analysis, research gaps, and conclusion.

Quantitative claims about the final academic corpus remain intentionally open until the peer-review audit and human calibration are complete. The historical retrieval numbers are kept separate from the final inclusion counts.
