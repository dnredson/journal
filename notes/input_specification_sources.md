## Editorial notes

- **Do not count the eight works as an exhaustive or random sample.** They are representative examples chosen to establish the analytic argument.
- **Do not add these eight to the historical corpus count without checking deduplication.** Their membership in blocks 1–12 has not been fully reconciled in this writing pass.
- **Do not infer corpus-wide trends from this subsection alone.** Claims about prevalence, gaps, and integration frequencies await the final peer-review gate and human-validated coding.
- Distinguish input perturbation, metric/evaluation signal, and downstream outcome. An improvement in final task accuracy alone does not establish general specification quality or cross-stage causal attribution.
- Use the publisher version of record for citations. The DOI and venue of every listed reference were checked against ACL Anthology or ICLR proceedings.
- Comparative table rows report **what each method demonstrates**, not an assignment of the final taxonomy's end-to-end integration class. Formal integration coding requires full-text adjudication.
