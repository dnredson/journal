\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}

The survey is organized around the following research questions:

\begin{description}
\item[RQ1:] \textbf{Which quality dimensions of LLM-based systems are evaluated in the existing literature?}

This question investigates the distribution of evaluation efforts across input/specification, context/retrieval, output, interaction, agent/process, and operational/system dimensions.

\item[RQ2:] \textbf{Which methods and evaluation protocols are used to assess these quality dimensions?}

We investigate deterministic metrics, reference-based and semantic metrics, human evaluation, learned evaluators, LLM judges, multi-judge approaches, execution-based evaluation, adversarial methods, trace-aware evaluation, and related strategies.

\item[RQ3:] \textbf{To what extent does the literature integrate quality assessment across multiple stages of an LLM-based system?}

We distinguish isolated evaluation, explicit cross-stage analysis, black-box end-to-end assessment, and lifecycle-integrated evaluation.

\item[RQ4:] \textbf{How is the reliability of the evaluation mechanism itself assessed?}

This question considers evaluator agreement, consistency, bias, calibration, robustness, human alignment, and other forms of meta-evaluation.

\item[RQ5:] \textbf{How are operational constraints incorporated into LLM quality assessment?}

We analyze the treatment of latency, throughput, computational resources, monetary cost, availability, observability, privacy, safety, and related system-level characteristics.

\item[RQ6:] \textbf{What gaps remain toward reliable end-to-end quality assessment of LLM-based systems?}

Finally, we synthesize the relationships and omissions observed across the preceding questions and identify opportunities for integrated quality assessment.
\end{description}
